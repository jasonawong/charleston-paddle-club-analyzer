CHARLESTON PADDLE CLUB

Your replay, explained.

Explore

Tap the route or speed chart to seek. Use Select section, then drag across the chart to choose an interval. Press Loop to repeat that interval, or the whole window if none is selected.

Set your race window

The bundled recording includes staging and post-finish paddling. Until you set boundaries, all times and insights describe the full recording. Your selected window is not an official race result.

Keyboard controls

Space: play/pause. Left/right arrows: seek 10 seconds. Home/End: go to the selected window’s start/finish. Native slider controls also work.

Your data

Uploaded GPX files are parsed locally and never uploaded by this app. Notes and boundaries stay in this browser. Map requests go to OpenStreetMap and reveal the viewed map area. The bundled race file will be publicly accessible if you publish this site publicly.

Conditions & sources

The bundled Chattajack conditions are a partial historical snapshot, not live weather. Station observations are shown only for the matching recording date and region. Readings older than 65 minutes are marked unavailable; they are never carried through the rest of the race as current observations.

  • NWS Chattanooga / Lovell Field · partial observations captured October 24, 2026; the source page rolls forward.
  • Chattajack forecast context · anticipated water temperature, not a measurement.
  • TVA water levels & releases · race-hour readings not yet verified.
  • © OpenStreetMap contributors · online basemap, displayed with Leaflet.

Track quality

Loading track details…

Speed uses a 30-second centered distance/time window, shortened at the selected boundaries. GPS gaps are not assigned speed. Distant segments and gaps over 30 seconds are not connected for distance calculations. Coaching comparisons use equal-distance sections within the selected window and do not infer effort or technique.
