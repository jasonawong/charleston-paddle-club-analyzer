Choose your alignment

Elapsed time starts both selected windows at zero, even if they were recorded on different days. Actual clock time uses the original GPS timestamps. When markers overlap, their badges move slightly apart with short connectors to their actual positions. A team waits until its selected start and stays at its last position when its window ends.

Make a fair comparison

Set each team’s window to the actual race start and finish before assessing performance. Each distance is measured along its own track, so a distance difference is not a measured gap along a shared course. The table compares split durations only when the distances match.

Explore together

Play both tracks, drag either chart, tap a route, or use the shared slider. Tap a split time to seek to that team’s split start. Space plays or pauses; arrows seek ten seconds; Home/End jump to the comparison boundaries. Playback pauses when the tab is hidden.

Recording intervals and speed

The women’s Garmin track uses variable 1–11 second recording intervals. This page interpolates intervals up to 15 seconds for both teams. Larger gaps hold the last observed position and show no speed. Segment breaks and gaps over 30 seconds add no distance. Speed uses a centered 30-second window; missing speeds break the chart line.

Your recordings

Use either Load GPX button to replace that team’s track. File contents stay in your browser; uploaded tracks do not persist after reloading. Team names and selected windows save in this browser per recording. The two bundled examples are public; the women’s example retains coordinates and timestamps only, with health extensions omitted.

Map source

Online map requests reveal the viewed area to OpenStreetMap contributors. GPS routes, charts, and splits remain available if the basemap is unavailable.
