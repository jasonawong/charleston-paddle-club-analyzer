Your replay, explained.

Explore

The playback controls sit above the map and stay near the top as you scroll. Tap the route or speed chart to seek. Use Select section, then drag across the chart to choose an interval. Press Loop to repeat that interval, or the whole window if none is selected.

Set your race window

The bundled recording includes staging and post-finish paddling. Until you set boundaries, times and splits describe the full recording. Your selected window is not an official race result.

Keyboard controls

Space: play/pause. Left/right arrows: seek 10 seconds. Home/End: go to the selected window’s start/finish. Native slider controls also work.

Your data

Uploaded GPX files are parsed locally and never uploaded by this app. Race boundaries stay in this browser. Map requests go to OpenStreetMap and reveal the viewed map area. The bundled race file will be publicly accessible if you publish this site publicly.

Follow the splits

The current split is highlighted as playback advances or you seek. On laptops the splits sit below the blue playback summary. Selecting a split replays and loops that interval; the final incomplete split is labeled separately.

Speed uses a 30-second centered distance/time window, shortened at the selected boundaries. GPS gaps are not assigned speed. Distant segments and gaps over 30 seconds are not connected for distance calculations. Split times are measured within the selected race window.
